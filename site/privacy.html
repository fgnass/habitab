Habitab

Privacy

Habitab is a free, non-commercial hobby project. There are no ads, nothing is for sale, and there is no business behind it.

Who is responsible

Felix Gnass · E-Mail

The app

Habitab runs only on your computer. It shows the browsers of your coding agents in one window.

  • No page, screenshot, note or other content is sent to me or to a server of mine.
  • The app has no analytics, no crash reports and no update checks.
  • The tabs are a normal browser: pages that you or your agent open load from their sites, as in Chrome. Text in the address bar that is not a URL goes to a Google search.
  • On Linux, the spell check in the text fields of pages can download a dictionary from Google's Chromium servers. On macOS, the spell check of the system is used.

What stays on your computer

Habitab keeps its data in ~/Library/Application Support/Habitab (Linux: ~/.local/share/habitab). Only your user can open this folder.

  • By default, each session keeps its cookies, storage and cache in memory. When the session ends, they are deleted.
  • So that running sessions come back after an app restart, their tabs and cookies are kept in sessions.json.
  • The timeline keeps screenshots of the agent's tabs. They are deleted when the session ends.
  • Log files record what the app does, for example the URLs of downloads. Downloads go to your Downloads folder.
  • A persistent profile for a project is off by default. When you turn it on, that project's cookies, storage and cache stay on disk until you delete them.
  • Setup writes one entry in the global config of chrome-devtools-mcp and keeps a backup of the file. Uninstall removes the entry.

Dictation

On a Mac, you can dictate a note. Recognition uses Apple's Speech framework and runs only on the Mac; Habitab requires on-device recognition. The microphone is on only while you hold the button. If a speech model for your language is missing, macOS can download it from Apple. Apple's part is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Your agent

When you send a note, a marked area or console errors, Habitab gives them to Claude Code or Codex on your computer. What these tools then send to Anthropic or OpenAI is covered by their privacy policies, not by this page. Other agents get the note on the clipboard.

This website

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • No accounts and no forms.
  • No fonts, scripts or other content from third-party servers. The only scripts are the copy button for the install commands and the e-mail link on this page. Links to other sites (for example GitHub) load nothing until you click them.

Hosting and install

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. The install commands load the source code from GitHub. During the build, npm loads packages from the npm registry (npm, Inc., part of GitHub), and Electron is downloaded from GitHub. When you open a page or install Habitab, your browser or your terminal sends the usual request data to these servers, including your IP address. GitHub keeps this data in server logs to run and protect the service. I have no access to these logs. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in delivering the website and the app reliably (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). For details, see GitHub's privacy statement.
